# Citing Claude (Harvard format)

If Claude helped you revise your exhibition, acknowledge it. Check your school's and the IB's current rules on AI assistance and which referencing style they require. These samples use the Harvard style (Cite Them Right pattern). The year, model version and date below are examples, so replace them with your own details.

## Template

**In-text:** (Anthropic, [year of the model's release])

**Bibliography:** Anthropic ([year of the model's release]) *Claude [model version]* [Large language model]. Available at: https://claude.ai (Accessed: [day month year you used it]).

## Sample

**In-text:** (Anthropic, 2026)

**Bibliography:** Anthropic (2026) *Claude Sonnet 5.5* [Large language model]. Available at: https://claude.ai (Accessed: 19 October 2026).

## Notes

- The model version is shown in the Claude app, and the model name is also in its answers if you ask. Use the version you actually used.
- Put the bibliography entry in alphabetical order with your other sources (by "Anthropic").
- Some schools ask you to describe how you used AI (for example, "checked sentence clarity and suggested cuts") or to keep your chat history. Do what your school requires.
- Cite the textbook and any other sources you used separately. Claude's help doesn't replace citing them.
